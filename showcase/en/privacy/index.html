Privacy policy for content-ai.net

Short and to the point: what we collect, why, and where we keep it. This covers the content-ai.net product site and the “Request access” form.

As of 3 October 2026

01Who is responsible for the data

The data controller is [TO BE COMPLETED (DO UZUPEŁNIENIA): company name or the name of the business owner, address, contact email].

02What the form collects

Your first name, email address, optionally a company or team name, the chosen plan, an optional message, the page language and the fact that the consent box was ticked. The server adds the date and time, your IP address and the address of the page the request came from.

03Why

Only to answer the access request, create an account and agree on a plan. The legal basis is your request and your consent to be contacted (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and (b)) [TO BE COMPLETED (DO UZUPEŁNIENIA): confirm the legal basis]. Providing the data is voluntary, but without a name and email address we cannot reply. We do not send newsletters and do not share the data for advertising.

04Where it is stored

The form sends the data straight to the Content AI server (app.content-ai.net), the same one that runs the app. We keep requests for 365 days after they are sent, then the server deletes them. Data of a created account is kept for as long as the account exists. Recipients: the server provider [TO BE COMPLETED (DO UZUPEŁNIENIA): name of the server provider] and the Cloudflare network, which delivers the content-ai.net site and processes technical connection data such as IP addresses in doing so [TO BE COMPLETED (DO UZUPEŁNIENIA): basis for transfers outside the EEA].

05What the site does not do

It loads no scripts, fonts or analytics from third-party servers and sets no cookies. Your light or dark theme choice is saved only in your browser and is never sent anywhere.

06Contact and your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict its processing, to data portability and to object. You can withdraw your consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (Prezes UODO). For data matters, write to [TO BE COMPLETED (DO UZUPEŁNIENIA): contact email] or use the same form, in the “Message” field.
