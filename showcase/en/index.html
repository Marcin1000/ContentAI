06AI reliability

Built around the ways AI gets things wrong.

A language model does not raise an error; it just keeps writing. So every typical failure has its own safeguard here.

#ProblemWhat Content AI does
01Invented figuresNumbers are required only when the sources contain them, and the fact check compares each figure with the documents.
02Filling gaps from memoryThree outcomes are allowed: the knowledge base, a search result listed as the source, or dropping the claim.
03Fabricated linksAddresses are copied from the sources character for character, and every link in a finished draft is checked twice: against the sources and live from the server.
04Facts stretched beyond the sourceScope is pinned to what the source says: one country, one product, one kind of customer.
05Outdated yearEvery prompt receives today's date, read from the clock at call time.
06Malformed responsesA repair pass resolves unclear spots the way a reader would, and shows a clear message when it cannot.

Each of these fixes is pinned in automated checks: removing or reverting one stops the release.

07Security

Keys and data under control.

Here is exactly what stays in your browser, what reaches the Content AI server and what goes to the AI provider.

  • Only your browser keeps your API key

    You save the key in the app, and your browser keeps it in an encrypted cookie that no script on the page and no other account can read. With each request it travels over an encrypted connection to the Content AI server, which passes it to the AI provider. The server does not save the key to disk or to logs and does not use it for anything else.

  • Model costs on your own account

    Every model request is billed to your account with the provider, not ours. You set a spend limit in the provider console and can see the estimated cost of each draft in the app.

  • Passwords and sessions

    Passwords are hashed with scrypt and a random salt. After 8 failed attempts from one address, sign-in is blocked for 15 minutes. The session lives in a signed HttpOnly cookie.

  • Only your account sees your documents

    Your knowledge base documents are stored on the Content AI server and only your account can see them. When writing, the model provider receives only the passages that match the topic.

  • Payments through Stripe

    You enter your card details on Stripe's page. Content AI never sees or stores them; from Stripe it receives only the payment status and the details needed to account for it.

  • Nothing from third-party servers

    The app loads no scripts or fonts from outside: libraries and typefaces come from the same server. The server has zero npm dependencies.

10FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How is Content AI different from an AI chat?

A chat answers from the model's memory. Content AI writes from your knowledge base, goes to the web only for missing information and cites it, then scores the draft for SEO, AIO, AEO and GEO and checks the facts. All in one workspace, from topic to publication.

What do I need to get started?

A Content AI account (email and password) and an Anthropic API key, which powers your drafts. You need the key on every plan, including Free. OpenAI keys (images, voice-over, transcription) and ElevenLabs keys (better voice-over) are optional. The Free plan gives you 3 articles to start, no card needed.

How do I get an API key, and what does the model cost?

In the Anthropic console you create an account, add credit and create a key; it takes about 10 minutes. You pay the provider for the model on your own account, roughly $0.25-0.50 for an article of about 800 words with scores and a fact check. A step-by-step guide and the calculation are on the “How to get an API key” page.

Where does my API key go?

Only your browser keeps it, in an encrypted cookie bound to your account; no other account can read it. With “Remember on this device” the key stays for 30 days, also after you sign out; without it, until you close the browser. It is removed by the “Remove key” button, signing out on all devices, changing or resetting your password and deleting your account. With each request the Content AI server passes the key to the AI provider and does not save it to disk or to logs.

Where do the facts come from?

From the knowledge base. With web search on, missing information comes from the pages listed under the article. Information about your company always comes from the base only.

Will the text be error-free?

No AI tool can guarantee that. Content AI reduces the risk: figures and links come from the sources, and the fact check lists the sentences the sources do not support, quoting each one. A person always has the final say.

How do I pay for a plan?

You pay for paid plans through Stripe, monthly in advance, in euros or Polish złoty: you choose the currency in the purchase window. You enter your card details on Stripe's page, and Content AI never sees them. Paid plans are available to customers with a billing address in the European Union. After each payment Stripe emails you a confirmation.

What document do I get after paying?

After each payment Stripe emails you a payment confirmation. We issue a receipt on request: write to kontakt@content-ai.net within 3 months of the end of the month in which you paid, giving the payment date. Prices on the site are final prices.

How do I change or cancel my plan?

In the account settings of the app you open the Stripe billing portal: there you can change your plan or card and cancel your subscription. After cancelling, your plan stays active until the end of the paid month, then your account moves to the Free plan, and your text history and documents stay. Within 14 days of purchase you can also withdraw from the contract: see the withdrawal information.

How does Content AI handle the EU AI Act?

The publisher is responsible for the publication: realistic images and recordings, and texts informing the public on matters of public interest without editorial review, must be labelled as AI-generated. For voice-over, the app says the voice is synthetic. Who is responsible for what is described on the “AI Act and transparency” page.

Where do my documents go?

Knowledge base documents are stored on the Content AI server and only your account can see them. When writing, the model provider receives only the passages that match the topic. Finished texts and their history stay in your browser.

Which AI models does Content AI use?

Drafts, scores and the fact check come from Anthropic models. Images, voice-over and transcription use OpenAI models, and the better voice-over uses ElevenLabs. Each provider bills usage to your own API key.

Where does the SERP data come from?

The Google results analysis is run by the model with web search, on your API key. It is an estimate of the results, not a ranking measurement. SERP analysis is available from the Standard plan up.

My account was created by an administrator. What changes?

You sign in as before, and your plan, limits and documents stay. Your drafts still run on the server keys, so you do not need your own key or a paid plan. You can still enter your own key if you prefer to pay for the model on your own account.

Which languages does Content AI support?

The interface is in Polish and English. Content can be written in Polish, English, German and Czech.
